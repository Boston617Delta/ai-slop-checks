# Banned AI copy patterns: a checklist

A plain version of the `ai-copy-tells` skill for anyone who doesn't use Claude Code. Paste it into ChatGPT or Claude along with your draft, or keep it next to your editor.

**Use it for:** landing pages, ads, emails, cold outreach, social posts, video scripts, sales pages, popups, and bios.
**Skip it for:** reports, audits, and other analysis, where careful qualification is accuracy, not weakness.

## The checks

- [ ] **No parallel repetition stack.** No two or more lines in a row that open with the same word or reuse the same sentence skeleton ("More leads. More calls. More revenue."). Each line should add something new. Check this one first.
- [ ] **No false contrast.** "It's not X. It's not Y. It's Z."
- [ ] **No three short sentences in a row.** Vary the rhythm.
- [ ] **No rhetorical section openers.** "But what does that actually mean?" Use a statement instead.
- [ ] **No "imagine" openers.** Open with a fact, a direct challenge, or a specific number.
- [ ] **No filler transitions.** "Let's dive in," "Here's the thing," "At the end of the day," "In today's fast-paced digital world," "Whether you're a X or a Y," "More on that later," "But wait, there's more."
- [ ] **No hedging in persuasive copy.** Cut "might," "could potentially," "in many cases," "often," and "usually," and back the claim with proof. If you can't prove it, cut it. Regulated claims (medical, legal, financial, insurance) keep the qualification their rules require.
- [ ] **No emoji** unless the platform expects them.
- [ ] **Almost no exclamation points.**
- [ ] **No dense five- or six-sentence paragraphs.**
- [ ] **Optional:** no em dashes.

## A prompt to run it with AI

```text
Check the draft below against this list of AI writing patterns. For each sentence that trips one, quote it, name the pattern, and give a rewrite that keeps my meaning and facts. Don't add claims, numbers, or testimonials I didn't provide. Don't rewrite sentences that pass. At the end, count the hits by pattern.

[Paste the checklist above, then your draft.]
```
